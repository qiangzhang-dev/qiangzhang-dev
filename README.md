# Qiang (Nate) Zhang

**Speech LLM Engineer · Supervised Fine-Tuning**

I work on **supervised fine-tuning (SFT) for speech interaction** at **iFLYTEK**. My work involves preparing and refining training data, fine-tuning models, and analyzing how training changes their responses. I focus on making responses more natural while preserving reasoning and general capabilities.

I use the team's existing evaluation workflows to compare model versions and make targeted fixes to scripts and processes as needed. Previously, my research at Tianjin University focused on **image restoration and fusion**.
